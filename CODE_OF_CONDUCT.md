# Code of Conduct

Squiggle is used by children. That is the whole reason this document is short
and blunt rather than long and legal.

This applies everywhere the project lives: GitHub issues and pull requests, the
Discord server, and the site itself.

## What's expected

- **Be kind to beginners.** Someone's first pull request, first bug report, or
  first question about how to run the thing gets a helpful answer, not a sigh.
- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This drops the room snapshot when
  two people save at once" is review. "Did you even test this" is not.
- **Keep it appropriate for a ten-year-old.** No sexual content, no gore, no
  slurs, no drugs, no dunking on people for being young or new.
- **Assume the other person means well** until they clearly don't.
- **Respect a no.** If someone asks you to drop something or stop contacting
  them, that's the end of it.

## What gets you removed

Harassment, sexual content or advances of any kind, targeting a minor,
threats, doxxing, hate speech, deliberately breaking or defacing other people's
projects and rooms, scam or malware links, and evading a ban.

Anything involving a child's safety is not handled with a warning. The account
goes, and where appropriate it gets reported to the platform.

## Reporting

- **In Discord:** ping a moderator, or use the report flow in the server.
- **On the site:** the report button on a project or a room.
- **On GitHub or anywhere else:** email **nbrewer@lumina-erp.com**.

Reports are read by a human and kept private. You will not be punished for
reporting something in good faith, even if it turns out to be nothing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, projects, or accounts
that break these rules, with or without a warning depending on severity. Normal
first-time friction gets a private word; the list above gets a removal.

If you think a decision was wrong, email the address above and say so. It will
be looked at again.
